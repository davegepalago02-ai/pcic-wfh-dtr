Install PCIC WFH on Mobile Phone

PCIC WFH Attendance Portal

Install as a standalone home screen app on your Android or iPhone. Fast access, works offline, and no browser address bar.

Android (Chrome, Edge, Samsung Internet)
  1. Open this website in Google Chrome or your mobile browser.
  2. Tap the three dots menu (⋮) in the top-right corner.
  3. Select "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
  4. Tap Install to confirm. The PCIC WFH icon will appear on your phone's home screen!
iPhone / iPad (Apple Safari)
  1. Open this website in Safari on your iPhone.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up ) at the bottom toolbar.
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen" ().
  4. Tap Add at the top right. You can now launch it directly from your iPhone home screen!
